Our story · Bothell's cantina corner

Taqueria restraint,
barrio bar embrace

La Gloria opened quietly on 17th Ave SE with one stubborn idea: Bothell deserved a modern Mexican bar with real depth — a kitchen that treats masa like a craft and a bar that respects agave the way a wine room respects vintage. Our tortillas are pressed by hand every hour. Our consommé simmers from morning till night. And on Fridays and Saturdays the dining room melts into a cantina bar that hums till 2 AM.

Friday nights bring a live DJ at the señora's polished-wood bar, where a bartender will pour you a smoked margarita from one of more than thirty mezcals and tequilas — then sit you back down with a bowl of consommé and fresh tortillas before the last song. It is neighborhood, it is late, and it is never rushed.

  • Trompo & plancha, fired hourly
