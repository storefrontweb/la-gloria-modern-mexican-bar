03 · La Casa

A cantina that keeps Bothell's late nights warm

La Gloria Modern Mexican Bar is a family-run cantina in north Bothell, a few minutes from Canyon Park and just off the 405 at 22010 17th Ave SE. The room pulls double duty — proper lunch service at table pace, then Friday nights that run on mezcal and good company until closing.

The kitchen presses tortillas to order, chars its salsas over live flame, and braises beef slow enough that the birria falls apart at a touch of the fork. The bar keeps north of thirty agave spirits, a salt-and-lime philosophy, and a margarita we're quietly proud of.

  • Tortillas pressed to order, every service, all week.
  • A bar list anchored on Oaxacan agave — mezcal, tequila, sotol.
  • Open late: Friday and Saturday till 2 AM.
  • Family-run, bilingual, and glad you found us.
