02 The house

Why Bothell stays up for a tortilla

La Gloria opened on the quiet corner of 22010 17th Ave SE, where Bothell's Main Street settles into the North Creek Valley. The idea was simple: a modern Mexican bar that doesn't fake the fire. Tortillas are pressed by hand each service, al pastor spins on the trompo, and the grill burns oak and charcoal deep into the evening.

No shortcuts here — and it shows in the rating. 4.7 stars across 348 Google reviews, where the word “al pastor” appears more often than anything else. Regulars order the birria on Tuesdays, the elote with extra lime, and a Mezcal Old Fashioned the instant dinner ends.

Most kitchens on this side of the lake lock the doors by nine. La Gloria keeps the comal hot until last call, seven days a week — a late-night kitchen for the neighborhood, from happy-hour margaritas to after-ten tacos.
