03 La Casa

A cantina built for Bothell's long evenings.

La Gloria is a modern Mexican kitchen and bar on 22010 17th Ave SE — a short stroll from downtown Bothell's shops, and a pleasantly dramatic drive from the rest of the Eastside. The idea is simple: real fire, honest chiles, and a bar that respects a proper margarita.

We cook from a short menu on purpose — six plates we'd order at last call — and we run it late. Come in at seven on a rainy Tuesday, take the window, let the smoke and citrus do their work. That 4.7 from 348 Google reviews is the neighborhood telling you what to do.

  • 01

    Carne asada, peppers and pineapple over live coals — never a flat-top shortcut in this kitchen.

  • 02

  • 03
